The first release of TailCopy: a keyboard-first clipboard manager for Windows, macOS
and Ubuntu that keeps everything you copy one shortcut away. No account, no cloud, no
tracking.

This release is a complete clipboard manager **on one device**. Syncing your history
between your own devices over your Tailscale tailnet is the next milestone; until then,
nothing you copy leaves the device it was copied on.

![The History window](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/moorew/tailcopy/v0.1.0/docs/images/history.png)

## Download

| System | File |
| --- | --- |
| Windows 10 (22H2) and 11, x64 | `TailCopy-0.1.0-windows-x64-setup.exe` |
| macOS 13 and later, Apple silicon and Intel | `TailCopy-0.1.0-macos-universal.dmg` |
| Ubuntu 24.04 and 26.04, GNOME on Wayland | `TailCopy-0.1.0-ubuntu-amd64.deb` (includes the GNOME extension) |

Install, open TailCopy and follow the setup; nothing needs a terminal.

- **Windows:** the installer isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen asks first. Choose
  **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **macOS:** setup asks for Accessibility so TailCopy can paste for you, with a button
  to the right pane.
- **Ubuntu:** setup turns on the GNOME extension and asks you to log out and back in
  once, with a button that does it.

Check downloads against `SHA256SUMS`.

## The picker

Press **Ctrl+Alt+V** (**⌘⇧V** on macOS) over any app, type a few letters, press
**Enter**: it pastes into the app you were using. **Shift+Enter** pastes plain text,
**Ctrl+1 to 9** pastes a row straight away, **Tab** shows a preview, and **Esc** clears
the search, then closes. Pinned items come first.

![The picker with its preview](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/moorew/tailcopy/v0.1.0/docs/images/picker.png)

Search is fuzzy and takes filters: `is:pinned`, `is:text`, `is:rich`, `is:image`,
`is:code`, `is:link`, `today`, `yesterday`. Or tap a chip.

![The picker in dark mode, showing code](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/moorew/tailcopy/v0.1.0/docs/images/picker-dark.png)

## Everything in this release

**Capture**
- Plain text, formatted text (HTML and RTF) and images, stored locally and searchable.
- Links, code and shell commands, colours and IP addresses are recognised: their own
  icons, code in a monospace font, colours shown as the colour, and filters for each.
- Copies of the same thing are grouped as one row with a ×N count.
- Single characters and blank copies are skipped (Settings > General to turn it off).

**History window**
- Library by type with live counts: All, Pinned, Code, Links, Images, Formatted.
- Search (Ctrl+F), filter chips, and items grouped as Just now, Earlier today,
  Yesterday and by day.
- A detail card with the full content, a label, where it was copied and how often
  you've used it.
- Multi-select to pin or delete several at once, Undo for 5 seconds after a delete,
  and Clear history (keeping pinned items unless you say otherwise).

**Pinned**
- Pinned items kept forever, shown as cards with how often you've used them, a menu to
  copy, unpin or delete, and first place in the picker.

![Pinned items](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/moorew/tailcopy/v0.1.0/docs/images/pinned.png)

**Privacy**
- Copies marked secret are never saved: Windows' clipboard-history exclusions, macOS
  concealed and transient types, and password-manager hints on Linux.
- 1Password, Bitwarden, KeePassXC and Proton Pass are ignored by name; add any app, or
  keep an app's copies on this device only.
- **Pause** for 15 minutes, 1 hour or until you resume, from the app or the tray; a
  timed pause resumes by itself.
- No accounts, servers, analytics, crash reports or update checks. Logs never contain
  anything you copied.

![Pausing capture](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/moorew/tailcopy/v0.1.0/docs/images/pause.png)

**Everywhere**
- Light and dark themes that follow your system, or pick one in Settings.
- Tray (menu bar on macOS) icon that shows when capture is paused and when Tailscale
  isn't running.
- Rebindable shortcuts, start at login, "Paste for me" or copy-only, and per-app paste
  keystrokes on Linux (terminals get Ctrl+Shift+V).
- A command line: `tailcopy picker`, `paste-plain`, `pause`, `resume`, `status`.
- GNOME on Wayland support through a small GNOME Shell extension installed with the
  app, so the global shortcut and paste-for-you work there too.
- Keyboard reach everywhere, screen-reader labels, AA contrast in both themes, and
  fades only when your system asks for reduced motion.

![Settings in dark mode](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/moorew/tailcopy/v0.1.0/docs/images/settings.png)

## Since the M1 test build

The whole app was redesigned: new colours, type, logo, app and tray icons; the History
window, picker and Settings rebuilt; and the Pinned shelf, timed pause, code/link
detection, use counts and Undo added. The sidebar counts that stayed at 0 are fixed.
Your history from the test build carries over.

## Known issues

- **macOS hasn't been tested on a real Mac yet.** It builds, signs and passes the
  automated checks, but please report anything odd.
- On Ubuntu the picker has square corners: GNOME's web view can't draw transparent
  windows.
- The `.deb` package is named `tail-copy`, so that's the name to use with `apt remove`.
- The device name and Settings > Tailnet are already there, but only matter once
  sync arrives.
