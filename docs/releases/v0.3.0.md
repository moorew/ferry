TailCopy 0.3.0 gets your computers ready for the TailCopy phone apps. Install it over
0.2.4; your history is kept.

Check downloads against `SHA256SUMS`.

## New

- **Pair a phone.** Settings > Tailnet > Phones > **Pair a phone** shows a QR code
  that the TailCopy app for iPhone and Android scans to pair with this computer. Pair
  once, and the phone finds your other computers and relay through it. The phone apps
  are still being tested and aren't available yet.
- **Device identity keys.** Each computer now creates its own key on first start, and
  the pairing code carries it. A phone uses it to check that it's talking to your
  computer and not just to something at the same address. Your computers still
  recognise each other through Tailscale, so there's nothing new to set up between them.
- **Phones in Devices.** A paired phone shows with a phone icon. While TailCopy isn't
  open on the phone, it reads "syncs while TailCopy is open on it" rather than offline.
- **Relay pairing.** `tailcopy-relay pair --data-dir DIR` prints a pairing code in the
  terminal.

Computers on 0.3.0 keep syncing with computers still on 0.2.x, so you can update them
one at a time.

If you're on 0.2.4, TailCopy tells you about this update by itself.
