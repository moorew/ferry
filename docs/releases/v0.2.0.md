TailCopy 0.2 syncs your clipboard history between your own devices, over your own
Tailscale tailnet. Copy on your laptop, paste on your desktop. No account, no cloud,
nothing in between: devices talk to each other directly, and only devices signed in
to your Tailscale account can connect.

Sync is new. Please report anything that goes missing, arrives twice, or shows up
where it shouldn't.

Install over 0.1; your history carries over. Check downloads against `SHA256SUMS`.

## Getting devices to sync

1. Install TailCopy 0.2 on each device.
2. Make sure Tailscale is running on each one, signed in to the same account.
3. That's it. Within a few seconds each device lists the others in
   **Settings > Tailnet**, and anything you copy shows up everywhere.

On Windows, the first time sync starts, Windows Firewall may ask whether TailCopy can
accept connections. **Allow it on private networks.** If you don't, that PC still
syncs by connecting out to your other devices.

If your tailnet policy isn't allow-all, let your devices reach each other on
`tcp:7441`.

## What's new

**Sync**
- Everything you copy, pin, label, delete or clear shows up on your other devices.
- Live: a copy on one device is in the picker on the others a moment later, and on
  their clipboard too, ready to paste. Something you copied more recently is never
  overwritten, and a device catching up after sleep never touches your clipboard.
- Catch-up: a device that was off or asleep gets everything it missed, in order, when
  it's back. Devices also pass along what they got from each other, so two devices
  that can't reach each other directly still sync through a third.
- Images sync too, up to 10 MB (change the limit, or turn images off, in
  Settings > Tailnet). Larger images stay on the device they were copied on.
- Only your devices: TailCopy listens only on your Tailscale addresses and checks every
  connection with Tailscale first. Only untagged devices signed in to your own
  Tailscale login can sync; anyone else on a shared tailnet is refused and listed in
  Settings.
- Passwords and secrets are still never stored, so they never sync. Apps you set to
  "keep on this device" stay local.

**Settings > Tailnet**
- Your devices running TailCopy, each with how it's connected (direct, or relayed
  through Tailscale), when it last synced, and **Send** and **Receive** switches.
  Receive off keeps clips from that device out of your history here; Send off keeps
  your clips' content from going to it directly. Both apply to new clips.
- Your other tailnet devices that don't have TailCopy yet.
- Image sync limit, "Put arrivals on the clipboard", "Only my devices", and an optional
  relay address.

**Everywhere**
- Clips that just arrived from another device are tinted, with a "From <device>"
  badge, in the picker and the History window.
- The tray icon shows a dot when something new arrives, until you open the picker or
  the window.
- The status card says how many devices you're synced with.
- `tailcopy status` and Copy diagnostics list your devices and their sync state.

**Fixes**
- Clearing history on one device can no longer be undone by a pin made on another
  device while it was offline.

## Known issues

- **Sync is new.** It's been tested between Windows and Ubuntu machines and by
  automated runs of real sync sessions; please report what you see on yours.
- On Ubuntu, clips that arrive go on the clipboard as plain text or an image; paste
  them from the picker to get formatting.
- Turning Send or Receive back on doesn't bring across clips made while it was off.
- macOS hasn't been tested on a real Mac yet.
- The `.deb` package is named `tail-copy`, so that's the name to use with `apt remove`.
