TailCopy 0.2.2 adds update notices and protects your history when your Tailscale
account changes. Install it over 0.2.1; your history is kept.

## Download

| System | File |
| --- | --- |
| Windows 10 (22H2) and 11, x64 | `TailCopy-0.2.2-windows-x64-setup.exe` |
| macOS 13 and later, Apple silicon and Intel | `TailCopy-0.2.2-macos-universal.dmg` |
| Ubuntu 24.04 and 26.04, GNOME on Wayland | `TailCopy-0.2.2-ubuntu-amd64.deb` (includes the GNOME extension) |

Check downloads against `SHA256SUMS`.

## What's new

- **Update notices.** Once a day TailCopy checks GitHub for a newer version. When one
  is out you get a notification, a "Download TailCopy …" item at the top of the tray
  menu, and an "Update to …" button in the History window. It opens the right installer
  for your computer. Nothing about you or your clipboard is sent, and you can turn it
  off in Settings > General.
- **Your history stays with your Tailscale account.** If this computer signs in to a
  different Tailscale account (or switches tailnet), sync stops so your history doesn't
  go to that account's devices. Settings > Tailnet explains and lets you allow the new
  account. Signing back in to your own account resumes sync by itself.

## Also

- The release pipeline now keeps the macOS signing keys in a protected environment that
